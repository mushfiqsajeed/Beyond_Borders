# 🌍 Beyond Borders — A Global Scholarship & University Guidance Portal

**Beyond Borders** is a global scholarship and university guidance portal designed to help students discover funding opportunities, evaluate profile qualifications, check admission criteria, and manage applications.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Backend Framework:** Python / Django (Implemented with Raw SQL via `django.db.connection` — *No Django ORM*)
* **Database:** MySQL (Managed via XAMPP)
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3
* **Version Control:** Git & GitHub

---

## ✨ Key Features & Modules

1. **Scholarship Search Portal:** Students can explore scholarships by selecting their preferred country, university, degree level, or field of study to discover matching funding opportunities[cite: 1].
2. **Student Profile Evaluation:** After entering academic information such as CGPA or GPA and educational background, students receive personalized recommendations for universities and scholarships[cite: 1].
3. **Admission Requirement Checker:** Centralized presentation of admission requirements, including required CGPA, entrance exams, application documents, and eligibility criteria[cite: 1].
4. **Standardized Test Comparison:** Detailed comparisons of exams like IELTS, TOEFL, GRE, GMAT, SAT, ACT, and Duolingo to help identify test purposes and target university acceptances[cite: 1].
5. **University Comparison Tool:** Side-by-side comparison of multiple universities based on tuition fees, global rankings, available scholarships, course offerings, and living expenses[cite: 1].
6. **Scholarship Eligibility:** System analyzes student profiles to identify eligible scholarships, filtering search results to display only relevant funding opportunities[cite: 1].
7. **Application Deadline Tracker:** Organized dashboard for tracking important deadlines for university admissions and scholarship applications[cite: 1].
8. **Cost Estimation Calculator:** Provides approximate cost breakdowns for tuition fees, accommodation, food, transportation, health insurance, and living expenses by country and university[cite: 1].
9. **Document Checker:** Secure platform for sharing CVs, Letters of Recommendation (LOR), and Statements of Purpose (SOP) with professional mentors for feedback and guidance[cite: 1].
10. **Country Study Guide:** Detailed profiles of study destinations including visa requirements, living expenses, work opportunities, climate, healthcare, and student lifestyle[cite: 1].
11. **FAQ & Guidance Center:** Answers common questions regarding scholarships, admissions, visa procedures, and studying abroad with step-by-step application guidance[cite: 1].
